This is a template. It is not legal, tax, or labor advice. Have a California lawyer, a Brazilian advogado, and a tax advisor review it before either Party relies on it.

## 1. Engagement

Diago engages the Contractor as an independent contractor to perform the services described in the statement of work signed with this Agreement (the **SOW**). The SOW is part of this Agreement. If the SOW and this Agreement conflict, the SOW controls on scope, rate, and dates, and this Agreement controls on everything else.

The Contractor starts on the date in the SOW and continues until this Agreement ends under Section 11.

## 2. How the work is done

The Contractor controls the manner and means of the work. Diago may set the result, the acceptance criteria, and a reasonable deadline. Diago does not set the Contractor’s daily hours, does not require the Contractor to work only for Diago, and does not supervise the Contractor the way it would supervise an employee.

The Contractor:

- uses the Contractor’s own equipment, tools, and internet connection, unless the SOW says Diago will supply a specific item;
- may work from Brazil and may set the Contractor’s own schedule, subject to deadlines in the SOW;
- may engage assistants at the Contractor’s own expense, and remains responsible for their pay, taxes, and conduct;
- has no authority to bind Diago, sign contracts for Diago, or speak for Diago.

Nothing in this Agreement requires exclusive service. A later oral promise of exclusivity, a fixed shift, or employee benefits does not change this Agreement unless both Parties sign a written amendment.

## 3. Not employment

The Contractor is not an employee, partner, agent, or member of Diago. This Agreement is not a US W-2 job and it is not a Brazilian CLT employment contract (contrato de trabalho).

Diago will not withhold Brazilian payroll taxes, will not enroll the Contractor in US employee benefit plans, and will not provide vacation, 13th salary, FGTS, unemployment insurance, or other employee benefits.

The Contractor is responsible for the Contractor’s own taxes in Brazil and, if any apply, in the United States, including registration as an autônomo or the entity the Contractor uses, nota fiscal or RPA if the Contractor’s municipality requires it, and payment of IR, INSS, ISS, and any other amounts the Contractor’s own advisors say are due.

The Parties know that a Brazilian court or labor authority can look at the real facts (subordination, habituality, exclusivity, and fixed hours) and may still find an employment relationship. This paper does not prevent that. If the Parties want Diago to direct daily work the way an employer would, they will stop and get advice from a Brazilian labor lawyer, or use an employer-of-record, before that work starts.

## 4. Pay

Diago will pay the fees in the SOW. The currency is US dollars unless the SOW says otherwise.

The Contractor sends an invoice after the work covered by that invoice is done, using the invoice fields in `04-invoice-template.md`. Diago pays a correct invoice within fifteen (15) days after receipt, by Wise, Payoneer, or international wire to the account in the Contractor’s payment-details sheet.

Diago does not pay in cash and does not reimburse expenses unless the SOW lists them. The Contractor bears currency conversion costs, correspondent-bank fees on the Contractor’s side, and Brazilian taxes. Diago may subtract a bank fee that Diago’s own bank actually charges for the outgoing transfer, and will say so on the payment record.

Diago may hold payment until the Contractor has delivered a signed Form W-8BEN that Diago can rely on, and until any invoice has a description that matches the SOW.

## 5. Taxes and Form W-8BEN

The United States and Brazil do not have an income tax treaty. The Contractor will give Diago a signed IRS Form W-8BEN (the current official form from irs.gov) before the first payment. The Contractor completes Part I and leaves Part II blank. The Contractor will not claim treaty benefits, and will not use Form 8233 to claim a treaty exemption, unless a tax advisor later instructs otherwise in writing because the law has changed.

The Contractor will tell Diago within ten (10) days if the W-8BEN becomes wrong.

Each Party will cooperate with reasonable information the other Party needs for a tax filing. Diago’s EIN is 42-3854578.

## 6. Ownership of the work

The Contractor assigns to Diago all right, title, and interest in the work product created under this Agreement (the **Work**), including copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, and other intellectual property, as soon as the Work is created. If a law does not allow an advance assignment of a particular right, the Contractor assigns that right as soon as the law allows, and appoints Diago to sign documents needed to perfect the assignment if the Contractor does not sign within ten (10) days after a request.

To the extent the law treats any Work as a work made for hire, it is a work made for hire owned by Diago. The rest of the Work is assigned as stated above.

The Contractor waives moral rights in the Work to the extent the law allows. Where the law does not allow a waiver, the Contractor agrees not to assert those rights against Diago’s use of the Work in Diago’s business.

The Contractor keeps ownership of materials the Contractor created before this Agreement or outside it (**Prior Materials**). The Contractor grants Diago a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use any Prior Materials that are included in the Work, for Diago’s business.

The Contractor will not put third-party code, content, or data into the Work unless the SOW allows that item and the license lets Diago use it for the intended purpose. The Contractor will not deliver Work that the Contractor does not have the right to assign.

Diago does not assign Diago’s name, brand, customer data, or existing products to the Contractor. The Contractor may mention the engagement as “independent contractor to Diago Technologies LLC” unless Diago asks the Contractor not to.

## 7. Confidentiality

**Confidential Information** means non-public information Diago discloses to the Contractor, or that the Contractor learns while performing the SOW, including product plans, source code, prompts, customer information, pricing, credentials, and this commercial relationship. It does not include information that the Contractor already knew without a duty to keep it secret, that becomes public other than by the Contractor’s breach, or that the Contractor receives from someone else who was allowed to disclose it.

The Contractor will use Confidential Information only to perform the SOW, will not disclose it to anyone except assistants who need it and are bound by written confidentiality terms at least as protective as this Section, and will protect it with at least reasonable care.

If a law or court requires disclosure, the Contractor may disclose the minimum required, after giving Diago prompt notice when the law allows notice.

These duties last for three (3) years after this Agreement ends. Trade secrets stay protected for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

On Diago’s request, or when this Agreement ends, the Contractor will return or delete Confidential Information and Diago’s materials, and may keep one copy only if the Contractor’s own lawyer or accountant requires it for a legal duty. The Contractor will also remove Diago credentials from the Contractor’s accounts and password manager.

## 8. Security

The Contractor will not share Diago credentials, will not send Diago secrets to a personal email or an unapproved chat tool, and will tell Diago promptly if the Contractor suspects unauthorized access to Diago systems or data. The Contractor will not store customer payment card data.

## 9. Privacy and customers

The Contractor will follow Diago’s written instructions when handling personal information Diago supplies. The Contractor will not contact Diago’s customers except as the SOW describes.

## 10. Warranties and indemnity

The Contractor represents that:

- the Contractor has the legal right to enter this Agreement and to work as an independent contractor from Brazil;
- the Work will be original or properly licensed, and will not knowingly infringe someone else’s intellectual property;
- the Contractor will perform the SOW in a professional and workmanlike manner.

The Contractor will indemnify Diago and its member against claims, losses, and reasonable attorneys’ fees arising out of (a) the Contractor’s taxes or a claim that the Contractor or the Contractor’s assistants are employees of Diago, (b) the Contractor’s breach of Sections 6 or 7, or (c) a third-party claim that Prior Materials or materials the Contractor supplied infringe that third party’s intellectual property. This indemnity does not apply to the extent the claim is caused by Diago’s modification of the Work or by Diago’s materials.

## 11. Term and ending the Agreement

Either Party may end this Agreement for any reason by written notice (email is enough) fourteen (14) days in advance. Either Party may end it immediately if the other Party materially breaches and does not cure within seven (7) days after written notice, or immediately if the other Party discloses credentials, refuses to assign Work already paid for, or becomes unable to perform.

When this Agreement ends, Diago pays for work the Contractor already performed and invoiced in accordance with the SOW, up to the end date. Sections 3, 5, 6, 7, 10, 12, and 13 survive.

## 12. Liability

Neither Party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, except for the Contractor’s breach of Section 6 or Section 7, the Contractor’s indemnity in Section 10, or a Party’s fraud or willful misconduct.

Except for those same items, each Party’s total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees Diago actually paid the Contractor under this Agreement in the three (3) months before the claim.

## 13. General

**Law.** California law governs this Agreement, without its conflict-of-law rules. The Parties submit to the state and federal courts located in California for disputes arising out of this Agreement. This choice of law does not strip either Party of rights that a Brazilian statute says cannot be waived. The Parties will try to resolve a dispute by email for fifteen (15) days before filing a claim, except either Party may seek an injunction to protect confidential information or intellectual property.

**No non-compete.** Diago does not ask the Contractor to agree to a non-compete. The Contractor may work for others. The Contractor may not use Diago’s Confidential Information while doing so.

**Entire agreement.** This Agreement and the SOW are the entire agreement on this subject. They replace earlier talks about the same engagement. Changes must be in writing and signed by both Parties (including electronic signature or a signed PDF by email).

**Assignment.** The Contractor may not assign this Agreement without Diago’s written consent. Diago may assign it to a successor of the Diago business.

**Severability.** If one provision is unenforceable, the rest stays in effect.

**Counterparts.** The Parties may sign separate copies, including electronic copies. English is the controlling language of this Agreement.
