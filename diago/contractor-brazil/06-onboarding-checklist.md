# Onboarding checklist (Diago keeps this)

Do not send the first payment until every required row is done.

Contractor: [CONTRACTOR_LEGAL_NAME]
Email: [CONTRACTOR_EMAIL]

| Done | Item |
|---|---|
| [ ] | Statement of work names the actual work, the rate, the currency, and the hour cap or delivery fee. |
| [ ] | Both Parties signed the Independent Contractor Agreement. |
| [ ] | Both Parties signed the Statement of Work. |
| [ ] | Contractor returned a signed official IRS Form W-8BEN. Part I is complete. Part II is blank. Citizenship and address are Brazil. Line 6a shows the CPF. |
| [ ] | W-8BEN name matches the agreement and the payment account. |
| [ ] | Payment-details sheet is signed, and the account holder is the contractor (or a business named in an amendment). |
| [ ] | A CPA has confirmed US withholding and whether any 1042-S filing is required for this kind of payment. |
| [ ] | Contractor knows to invoice, and not to call the payment salary. |
| [ ] | Access granted is limited to what the SOW lists. No shared personal passwords. |
| [ ] | Contractor was sent `07-carta-de-apresentacao-pt.md` so the Portuguese summary matches the English contract. |

## After every payment

| Done | Item |
|---|---|
| [ ] | Invoice number, period, and amount match the transfer. |
| [ ] | Description says services performed in Brazil. |
| [ ] | Copy of the invoice and proof of payment stored with this checklist. |

## Do not do these

- Do not put the contractor on Gusto, a W-2, or a US benefits plan under this package.
- Do not file a W-9 for this person.
- Do not promise vacation, 13th salary, FGTS, or exclusive full-time hours and still call it contracting.
- Do not send production credentials before the agreement is signed.
