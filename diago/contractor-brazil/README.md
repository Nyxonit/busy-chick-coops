# Diago contractor package — Brazil

Send this folder to the person in Brazil. It is an **independent contractor** package from **Diago Technologies LLC**. It is not a W-2 hire, and it is not a Brazilian CLT employment contract.

These are templates for you and your contractor to fill in. They are not a legal opinion, a tax opinion, or a substitute for a California lawyer, a Brazilian advogado, or a CPA. Have those people read the signed set before the first payment if the work will be ongoing, exclusive, or supervised day to day.

## Why this is a contractor package

Diago Technologies LLC is a California company. It has no Brazilian employer entity in this package. A person who lives in Brazil and does the work from Brazil should be engaged as a prestador de serviços (independent contractor), not put on a US payroll and not handed a CLT employment contract this company cannot perform.

Brazilian labor rules can still treat the relationship as employment if the real facts look like a job: fixed hours, exclusive work, orders about how to do the task, and a habitual paycheck. The English contract does not block that. If you need that kind of control, talk to a Brazilian labor lawyer or use an employer-of-record before work starts.

## Tax, in plain language

The United States and Brazil do **not** have an income tax treaty. Confirmed against the IRS treaty list: Brazil is not on it. The contractor still signs Form W-8BEN, Part I only, to certify foreign status. Part II (treaty benefits) stays blank. Do not use Form 8233 to claim a treaty exemption.

Services the contractor performs entirely in Brazil are generally foreign-source income, which is a different bucket from US-source dividends, interest, or royalties (those can face 30% US withholding with no treaty). A CPA should confirm the sourcing and whether Diago must file any information return before you rely on that.

The contractor handles Brazilian taxes (IR, INSS, ISS, and a nota fiscal or RPA if their city requires one). Diago does not withhold Brazilian payroll taxes and does not provide CLT benefits.

5. Wait for the signed agreement, the signed statement of work, a signed IRS Form W-8BEN (official PDF from irs.gov), and the payment-details sheet.
6. Pay only against an invoice, in the currency the statement of work names.
